Community ListServe

Stay connected with SCGIS DC

Our Google Group listserve is currently our main form of communication. Join to receive chapter announcements, upcoming event details, opportunities, and community updates directly by email.

Sign up for the SCGIS DC ListServe

How to sign up

  1. Open the SCGIS DC Google Group using the button above.
  2. Sign in to the Google account where you want to receive messages.
  3. Click Join group or Ask to join group.
  4. Choose your preferred email frequency and confirm your request.

Once you are approved, messages and announcements will arrive in your email inbox.

Chapter Leads

Maria Zubkova

Chapter Lead

Maria has been a Board member of the SCGIS since 2023. Prior to joining DC Chapter leadership, she spent 3 years chairing the Conference Committee. At her day job, Maria utilizes satellite data to monitor environmental changes throughout the globe as an Associate Research Professor in the Department of Geographical Sciences at the University of Maryland, College Park. Her favorite animal is a bear, any bear, all of them.

mzubkova@scgis.org

Abigail Pendry

Chapter Lead

Abigail stumbled into a DC SCGIS Happy Hour a month after moving to the DC area in 2020 and has been an avid member and evangelist since. During the day, Abigail designs and automates spatial analyses for the National Audubon Society. Her favorite plant to stop and stare at (and touch) while on a hike is moss.
